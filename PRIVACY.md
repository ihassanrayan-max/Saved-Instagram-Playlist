# Privacy policy - Instagram Video Playlist

Updated November 22, 2026.

Instagram Video Playlist organizes references to Instagram posts in your browser. It has no custom backend, analytics, advertising service, or extension account system.

## Data stored

The extension stores playlist names, post URLs and shortcodes, available thumbnails and creator/caption text, and titles, notes, and tags you enter. Collection progress and player position are stored locally in Chrome. Playback position, preferences, and readiness timings are held in browser-session storage.

This information supports collection, search, editing, and playback. The extension does not request, read, or store your Instagram password, authentication tokens, or cookies through extension APIs. Instagram handles its own signed-in session.

## Network access

Playing a post navigates to Instagram. Original thumbnails can contact the image hosts in those URLs, normally Instagram's media infrastructure. These requests expose normal browser request information to those services, which operate under their own policies. The extension does not upload your playlists or notes to a separate service or sell them.

No external analysis service receives your saved content or notes. Extension code is included in the installed package.

## Your controls

You can edit playlists, remove individual entries or playlists, and export/import your library as JSON. Exports include notes and post references; review them before sharing.

Local library data remains until removed or the extension is uninstalled. Collection checkpoints can be removed with **Clear Collected URLs**. Session state is temporary and is cleared when the browser session ends. Deleting an extension playlist does not unsave its posts on Instagram. Exported copies remain wherever you save or share them.

## Changes and contact

Changes to data handling will be reflected in this policy and release documentation. Contact the maintainer through the [project issue tracker](https://github.com/ihassanrayan-max/insta-saved-playlist/issues). Do not post passwords, cookies, or sensitive library contents in public issues.
